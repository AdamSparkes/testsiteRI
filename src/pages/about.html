About Rutter

Turning Radar Data Into Operational Decisions

For over thirty years, Rutter has engineered the software and signal-processing systems that help maritime operators see more, decide faster, and operate with greater confidence — in any condition, anywhere in the world.

Who We Are

Rutter Inc. was founded in St. John's, Newfoundland — a city shaped by the sea — and that environment has defined our engineering culture ever since. We started with a single objective: extract meaningful intelligence from radar signals that existing systems were discarding. That focus has never changed.

Today, the sigma S6 platform is the result of decades of refinement. It connects to radars already installed on a vessel or facility, processes raw returns through purpose-built algorithms, and delivers actionable data to the operators who need it most. No new infrastructure. No separate hardware ecosystem. Just better decisions from the equipment you already trust.

◆

Maritime-First Engineering

Every product we build is designed specifically for the maritime environment — engineered to perform in sea clutter, ice, reduced visibility, and high-traffic corridors where standard radar returns fall short.

◆

Passive Integration

Sigma S6 modules connect to your existing radar without modifications to the source equipment. Operators gain new capability without interrupting the systems they depend on.

◆

Expandable by Design

Start with one module and add others over time on the same processing unit. As operational requirements evolve, the platform scales with them — no rip-and-replace cycles required.

◆

Proven in the Field

Rutter systems are deployed on vessels and facilities operated by coast guards, offshore energy companies, cargo fleets, and research institutions across more than thirty countries.

Our History

A journey of exploration, engineering, and global expansion.

1998

Rutter Was Incorporated

Rutter Inc. is established in St. John's, Newfoundland, as a specialized private firm for ice detection and navigation, oil spill detection and tracking, and small target tracking in clustered seas.

2004

First Launch of Rutter Branded Radar Solutions

Rutter launches Oil Ice STS, marking the first commercial deployment of branded radar solutions built on the company's foundational signal-processing expertise.

2012

Acquisition of Ocean Waves GmbH

Rutter acquires Ocean Waves GmbH and its WaMoS II and surface current monitoring technologies, expanding the company's capabilities in wave and current analysis.

2014

Wave Fusion Launch

The Wave Fusion module launches, enabling operators to integrate multiple data sources for comprehensive wave monitoring and analysis.

2019

Wave Vision Launch

Wave Vision is released, bringing advanced visualization and analytical capabilities to wave and current monitoring across maritime operations worldwide.

2020

Current Monitor Launch Announcement

Rutter announces the Current Monitor module, delivering real-time surface and sub-surface current tracking for enhanced maritime operational awareness.

2020

Canadian Ocean Supercluster Partnership

Rutter launches an Internet of Things project in partnership with the Canadian Ocean Supercluster, accelerating innovation in connected maritime systems and ocean technology.

2021

Wave Signal Launch Announcement

Wave Signal is announced, introducing next-generation signal processing for maritime applications and further expanding Rutter's product portfolio.

2021

CCG Ice Hazard Contract Signed

Rutter signs a significant contract with the Canadian Coast Guard for ice hazard detection and monitoring solutions across Canadian waters.

2023

Ørsted Partnership Established

Rutter establishes a strategic partnership with Ørsted, a global energy company, to integrate radar intelligence into offshore wind farm operations and maritime safety.

2024

Econext International Business Award

Rutter receives the Econext International Business Award, recognizing the company's innovation and excellence in maritime technology and global business leadership.

2026

Slip Sense Launch Announcement

Rutter announces Slip Sense, the latest innovation in radar-based monitoring solutions, marking the company's continued commitment to cutting-edge maritime technology.
