About Rutter

Turning Radar Data Into Operational Decisions

For over thirty years, Rutter has engineered the software and signal-processing systems that help maritime operators see more, decide faster, and operate with greater confidence — in any condition, anywhere in the world.

Who We Are

Rutter Inc. was founded in St. John's, Newfoundland — a city shaped by the sea — and that environment has defined our engineering culture ever since. We started with a single objective: extract meaningful intelligence from radar signals that existing systems were discarding. That focus has never changed.

Today, the sigma S6 platform is the result of decades of refinement. It connects to radars already installed on a vessel or facility, processes raw returns through purpose-built algorithms, and delivers actionable data to the operators who need it most. No new infrastructure. No separate hardware ecosystem. Just better decisions from the equipment you already trust.

◆

Maritime-First Engineering

Every product we build is designed specifically for the maritime environment — engineered to perform in sea clutter, ice, reduced visibility, and high-traffic corridors where standard radar returns fall short.

◆

Passive Integration

Sigma S6 modules connect to your existing radar without modifications to the source equipment. Operators gain new capability without interrupting the systems they depend on.

◆

Expandable by Design

Start with one module and add others over time on the same processing unit. As operational requirements evolve, the platform scales with them — no rip-and-replace cycles required.

◆

Proven in the Field

Rutter systems are deployed on vessels and facilities operated by coast guards, offshore energy companies, cargo fleets, and research institutions across more than thirty countries.

Our History

A narrative arc of confirmed milestones, from pre-Rutter heritage through the next planned release.

1998

Rutter Was Incorporated

Rutter Inc. is incorporated in St. John's, Newfoundland, creating the formal company base that commercialized the heritage technologies.

1998

Ice Detection and Navigation

Developed under Sigma Engineering before incorporation. This heritage capability later became foundational to Ice Navigator, and is not treated as a Rutter-era launch.

1998

SeaFusion

Early multi-layer radar fusion concepts established in the heritage period, later reintroduced as Fusion in 2014.

2003

Surveillance

First commercial Surveillance deployments occur before standardized product packaging existed, marking early Rutter commercialization.

2006

Workforce Expansion Marker

Internal workforce expansion around 2006 corroborates that commercial Surveillance activity predates this year.

2009

Oil Spill Detection

First confirmed sale to Castalia Ecolmar, anchored by purchase-order evidence and treated as the strongest early commercial marker.

2012

Acquisition of OceanWaveS GmbH

Rutter brings radar-based wave and current measurement in-house. WaMoS lineage traces to OceanWaveS heritage work dating to the late 1990s.

2014

WaMoS® II

WaMoS® II becomes a Rutter product during a major portfolio expansion phase.

2014

Fusion

SeaFusion concepts are reintroduced as Fusion in modern product form.

2014

Current Monitor

Current Monitor enters its first market-facing form, aligned with the expanding wave and current measurement portfolio.

2014

Junior Achievement NL Business Hall of Fame

Fraser Edison is inducted, with recognition tied directly to leadership at Rutter.

2019

Turning the Tide Marine Industry Awards

Industry Lifetime Achievement Award to Fraser Edison, with citation explicitly referencing Rutter's global radar leadership.

2020

WaveVision

Released in the Sigma S6 era and now retired.

2020

WaveSignal

Released with forward-looking wave prediction capability for operational planning.

2020

Current Monitor

Reintroduced and repositioned as part of a broader modern Sigma S6 platform strategy.

2020

Canada's Ocean Supercluster IoT Project

Rutter leads a $3.1M co-funded initiative, signaling strategic shift toward cloud connectivity, remote monitoring, and radar-as-a-service delivery models.

2021

Canadian Coast Guard Contract

Contract awarded for sigma S6 Ice Navigator, providing major federal operational validation.

2023

Ørsted Partnership

WaveSignal deployment supports offshore wind crew transfer safety operations.

2024

Wärtsilä and Seaspan Collaboration

Collaboration supports the Canadian Coast Guard Polar Icebreaker Program.

2024

Unify (Web)

Web-based Unify component is released as part of platform modernization.

2024

econext International Business Award

Company-level recognition of international export performance and market impact.

2026 (TBD)

SlipSense

Planned roadmap item focused on bow slip awareness. Labeled future and pending confirmation of launch timing.
